live demo

Income Tax Agent

Ask a question about India's new Income-tax Act in plain English. The app finds the right sections of the 674-page law and answers only from them, with citations. If the law doesn't cover it, it says so.

  • Traced wrong answers to a root cause: amendment footnotes that quote repealed law. Wrote a PDF cleaner that strips them and their in-text markers across 553 printouts.
  • Built an eval harness with versioned run configs and used it for every design choice: dense vs. BM25 vs. hybrid search (RRF), section-title chunk prefixes, neighbour chunks and LLM query expansion.
  • Added a local cross-encoder reranker (ms-marco-MiniLM, 80 MB) that reorders the pooled results of the question and its rewrites. It was the biggest single gain: right section first went from 18 to 23 of 23 questions. A 96-run grid picked the app's setup, and the small reranker beat the 1 GB one.
  • Checked for overfitting with 10 held-out questions written after the prompts were frozen: right section first on 9 with the reranker, in the top 5 on all 10.
  • Tests run in CI with a fake LLM and fake embeddings, so they need no API keys or model downloads.

Reading the question or the room?

When an AI model looks at a photo of people and makes a gendered guess, is it reacting to the question or to the picture? Most bias benchmarks give one mixed score. My thesis separated the two.

  • Benchmarked 10 open VLMs (Qwen, Gemma, LLaVA) on SB-Bench, using vLLM for fast forward passes on a Slurm GPU cluster.
  • Built a FLUX.2 pipeline that swaps the background while keeping the people fixed, to isolate the scene's causal effect.
  • Wrote a sliding-window ablation of vision tokens with PyTorch hooks to find where in the image the bias starts.

Feb – Oct 2023
funded by ISRO

Band selection for hyperspectral images

Hyperspectral images have hundreds of colour bands per pixel, and most are redundant. This method uses Biogeography-Based Optimization to keep only the useful bands, then classifies each pixel with an SVM. Funded by the Indian Space Research Organisation.

  • Beat Genetic Algorithm and Differential Evolution baselines on accuracy at every band count tested, from 5 to 40 bands.
  • Extended the work to deep learning in a Springer book chapter: tested 2D, 3D, multi-dimensional and diverse-region CNNs on the selected bands across three datasets. Band selection cut the 3D CNN's trainable parameters by 86% and its training time by 69% on Indian Pines.
